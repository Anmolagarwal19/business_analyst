# Tata Power EV Charging Infrastructure Dashboard

A comprehensive Excel-based analytics dashboard designed to evaluate the performance, profitability, and expansion potential of electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure across districts.

## 📋 Project Overview

**Objective:**
Combine operational data, customer usage patterns, and financial ROI metrics to guide strategic investment decisions, optimize station performance, and identify high-potential locations for future expansion.

**Target Audience:**
- Senior Management & Strategy Teams
- Operations Managers
- Investment & Planning Departments
- Infrastructure Decision Makers

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### Page 1: Business & Usage Overview
Track charging demand trends, station capacity utilization, and hourly behavior patterns.

**Key Problems Addressed:**
- How is charging demand evolving over time?
- Are stations able to handle growing demand?
- What is the charging behavior pattern by time of day?

**KPIs Tracked:**
- Total charging sessions
- Total energy consumed (kWh charged)
- Total revenue (from charging sessions)
- Number of active customers
- Average revenue per session
- Average kWh per session

### Page 2: Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency
Identify overutilized and underutilized stations, analyze plug efficiency, and load distribution.

**Key Problems Addressed:**
- Which stations are overutilized and may require capacity expansion?
- Which stations are underutilized and need operational improvement?
- Are available plugs being efficiently used across locations?
- What is the load distribution across districts and stations?

**KPIs Tracked:**
- Number of sessions per station
- Average utilization per station (sessions per plug)
- Plugs count vs actual usage
- Average kWh per station
- District-wise station density
- Sessions per plug ratio

### Page 3: Customer & Demand Behavior
Segment customers, analyze usage patterns by income tier and EV model.

**Key Problems Addressed:**
- What type of customers contribute most to usage and revenue?
- How does income tier influence charging behavior?
- What are the usage patterns across different EV models and battery capacities?
- Are there segments with high potential but low engagement?

**KPIs Tracked:**
- Unique customers count
- Sessions per customer
- Average kWh per customer
- Revenue per customer
- Usage by income tier
- Usage by car model and battery capacity

### Page 4: Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy
Calculate ROI, payback periods, and identify priority locations for expansion.

**Key Problems Addressed:**
- Which districts and projects deliver the highest ROI?
- What is the payback period for different charging setups?
- How do costs such as CAPEX, land lease, and infrastructure impact profitability?
- Which locations should be prioritized for new station deployment?
- What is the gap between existing infrastructure and projected EV demand?

**KPIs Tracked:**
- Total CAPEX per project
- Annual revenue and EBITDA
- Payback period (with and without subsidy)
- 5-year ROI percentage
- Existing stations vs projected EVs
- Demand-supply gap (projected EVs vs projected plugs)
- Priority tier classification
